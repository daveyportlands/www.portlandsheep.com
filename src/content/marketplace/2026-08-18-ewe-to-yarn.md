We shear small to medium flocks of sheep in Somerset and parts of the surrounding counties. We will consider travelling further afield for group bookings.

Add-ons such as hoof trimming and drenching are available to shearing customers.

We are happy to discuss possible processing of your fleece and take commissions for spinning, knitting and weaving of your own fleece.
