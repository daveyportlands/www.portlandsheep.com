Carole Muddiman will be judging all three Premier Shows in 2025.

**Midland Premier** results are in — Champion: Hannah Stott with shearling ewe. Reserve: Tricia Robarts.

**Northern Premier** takes place at the Royal Lancashire Show, July 18–20.

**Southern Premier** takes place at the New Forest Show, July 29–31.
